## LinkedIn Strategy Framework

### Profile Optimization (Company + Personal Brands)
- **Company Page**: Banner, tagline, about section optimized for health tech + AI keywords
- **Executive Profiles**: CEO, CTO, sales team — positioned as thought leaders
- **Headline formula**: [Role] | Helping [audience] achieve [outcome] with [method]
- **About section**: Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA
- **Featured section**: Case studies, webinars, key content pieces

### Content Strategy
- **Post frequency**: 3-5x/week per active profile, 2-3x/week company page
- **Content mix**: 40% thought leadership, 30% case studies/proof, 20% engagement, 10% promotional
- **Formats**: Text posts (highest reach), carousels (highest saves), video (highest engagement), polls (algorithm boost)
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 per post, mix of broad (#HealthTech, #SaludDigital) and niche (#IAenSalud, #HistoriaClinicaElectronica)

### Content Pillars for Health Tech in LATAM
1. AI applied to healthcare — use cases, benefits, ROI
2. Digital transformation in hospitals and clinics — real stories
3. Interoperability and health data standards (HL7 FHIR, etc.)
4. Regulatory landscape in LATAM health tech
5. Patient experience and digital health
6. Behind-the-scenes of building health tech solutions

### LinkedIn Paid Campaigns
- **Campaign types**: Sponsored Content, Message Ads (InMail), Lead Gen Forms
- **Targeting**: Job titles (CTO, CIO, Director IT, Gerente Sistemas) + Industry (Hospital, Healthcare) + Geography (LATAM countries)
- **Budget allocation**: 70% lead gen forms, 20% content promotion, 10% brand awareness
- **A/B testing**: Always test 2+ ad variants per campaign
- **Lead Gen Forms**: Pre-filled fields, max 4 fields, strong CTA

### Social Selling Playbook
1. **Connect**: Personalized connection requests (no generic messages)
2. **Warm up**: Engage with prospect's content for 1-2 weeks before pitching
3. **Value first**: Share relevant content, insights, or introductions
4. **Soft CTA**: Invite to webinar, share case study, offer consultation
5. **Handoff to SDR**: When interest is confirmed, warm introduction

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator Tactics
- Build saved searches for ICP decision makers
- Set up lead alerts for job changes, company news
- Use TeamLink for warm introductions
- Track account engagement signals
- Export lead lists for ABM campaigns
